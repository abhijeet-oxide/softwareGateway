A pod in Init is WAITING, not failing. Every workload has a wait-for-<dependency>
init container, so ordered startup is visible as pods that have not begun:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs <pod> -c wait-for-database

WHAT A CHANGE COSTS

  a product, a policy    nothing restarts - all three watch their directories
  a person               one Job runs; no pod is touched
  config.yaml, an image  a rolling update with maxUnavailable {{ .Values.rollout.maxUnavailable }}

this serves anyone real.
